Terms

Lane
The kind of work this round: EXPAND finds new parents, IMPROVE adds records to people already in the tree, ROTATE re-reads a profile for anything new.
Generation
Counted from the couple the tree starts at (Gen 1). Gen 2 are their parents, Gen 3 grandparents, and so on.
PID
A person's id on FamilySearch. The tree uses its own ids (P-XXXXXX) and treats FamilySearch as one place to compare against.
ARK
A permanent link to one record (a census line, a baptism) on FamilySearch.
Limb (g)
A relative's record that names this person counts as a record of them: a child's baptism, marriage or death naming them as the parent, or any record where they appear as witness, sponsor, informant or declarant. It counts only once the name is shown to be this person (spouse, place, dates). A brother's or sister's record that does not name them counts for nothing.
Tier
How sure a claim is: strong signal, moderate signal, or speculative.
